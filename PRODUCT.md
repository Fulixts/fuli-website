# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and potential clients have equal priority. They visit to understand Gabriel Fuli's work and experience and find a way to contact him.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio presenting software engineering experience, technical practice, career history, education, and contact links.

## Operating Context

Visitors can read the site in English or Portuguese. The page is a single scrolling document with sections for introduction, background, web and front-end work, AI practice, career, stack, education, and contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The implementation is a static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript site. The page has separate PT and EN controls, responsive navigation, and a dark animated background. Preserve the existing content's factual claims unless the owner changes them. The owner's full name must not appear in visible site copy.

## Brand Commitments

Use Gabriel Fuli as the public name. Preserve the current monochrome, dark, code-inflected visual identity unless a later request changes it.

## Evidence on Hand

The current copy in `index.html` and its Portuguese translations in `main.js` describe work history, technologies, and contact destinations. Do not invent testimonials, client endorsements, or performance claims.

## Product Principles

- Let visitors understand the person and the work from the first screen.
- Keep English and Portuguese equally accessible.
- Make career and technical claims easy to scan without changing their meaning.
- Keep contact paths clear.
