About

I'm a computer engineer and have been building software since 2019. Today, at RDI Software, I ship new features and investigate critical incidents in McDonald's applications used in markets such as Germany, Australia, Canada and the United States.

I enjoy the work that demands calm: correlating logs across systems, reproducing the issue in a controlled environment, reaching the root cause and delivering a fix that breaks nothing in another country. I talk little and investigate a lot.

On the back end, Node.js is my tool of choice, alongside years of C# and .NET. I build and consume REST APIs, webhooks and OAuth 2.0 authentication, and connect systems, databases and services with n8n workflows.

AI in practice

I use generative AI as an engineering tool, not a showcase: to deliver faster, with more context, and with verification.

Agentic development
Daily work with Claude Code: I plan, delegate to agents, review and verify. The agent writes; accountability for the result stays with me.
Harness engineering
I build the environment agents operate in: skills, hooks, subagents, persistent memory and MCP servers, with quality gates before anything is called "done".
AI integrations and RAG
OpenAI and Anthropic APIs applied to client businesses, with Retrieval-Augmented Generation so answers stay grounded in the company's own data.
n8n automation
Workflows that connect APIs, systems and databases, removing repetitive manual work end to end.
Own product
I'm building Vibron, an Electron desktop IDE designed for working with AI agents, from editor to terminal.
